GV

Grahavani

Vedic astrology & AI astrologer · Flutter, Dart, Riverpod, Hive, Firebase AI (Gemini)

  • Android
  • Open source · Apache-2.0

The problem

Most Kundli apps need an account, send birth details to a server and charge for readings. I wanted a Vedic astrology app that calculates on the phone, keeps birth data private and costs nothing to run.

My role

Personal project, built end to end by me: product plan, calculation engine, UI, AI integration, tests and documentation. Source is public on GitHub.

Approach

  • Pure-Dart calculation engine: planets, Lagna, nakshatras, Navamsa (D9), Vimshottari dasha and Panchang, using the Lahiri ayanamsa. It runs fully offline.
  • Planet positions come from a compact Chebyshev table built from NASA/JPL DE440s, covering 1850–2150, and are tested against Swiss Ephemeris (planets within 3–4 arcseconds).
  • North and South Indian charts drawn with custom painters, plus planet table, dasha timeline, Avakhada and Manglik, Kaal Sarp and Sade Sati doshas.
  • Rule-based forecasts for Today, Week, Month and Year from Vedic transits, so the same chart always gives the same reading.
  • Jyotish Guru: Gemini through Firebase AI Logic, with streamed replies and voice input. It receives only the calculated chart, never the name, birth date, time or place. Daily free limits come from Remote Config.
  • Birth place search: an offline list of about 70,000 towns, plus online village search and current location, with the historical time zone worked out for each place.
  • Birth details stored only on the device (Hive). Google sign-in is optional; everything works as a guest.
  • Riverpod and go_router for state and navigation, with unit and widget tests for the engine, forecasts, chat and auth.
